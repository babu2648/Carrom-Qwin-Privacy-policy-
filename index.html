Welcome to Carrom Qwin ("we," "our," or "us"), developed and operated by Qbren Studio. We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring your personal information is protected. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we collect, how it is handled, and how your privacy is protected when you play our mobile game Carrom Qwin.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide

  • Account Data: If you choose to log in using Google Sign-In or Firebase Authentication, we receive your email address, display name, and avatar picture. If you choose to play as a Guest, an anonymous local identifier is created without collecting your personal identity.
  • Profile Photos: If you upload or change your profile picture from your device gallery, the game accesses only the specific photo selected. We never scan, upload, or index your photo library.

B. Automatically Collected Information & Gameplay Data

  • Game Progress & Stats: Level, trophies, rank, coin/gem balance, match outcomes, unlocked strikers, and game boards.
  • Device Diagnostics: Device model, OS version, language preference, and connectivity state to optimize game performance and prevent crashes.
  • Real-Time Matchmaking: IP address and temporary WebRTC session descriptors necessary for establishing peer-to-peer (P2P) connections during online multiplayer games.

C. Device Permissions

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: To sync game progress with cloud databases and enable online multiplayer matchmaking.
  • BILLING: To securely purchase optional in-game currency through Google Play's official billing system.
  • RECORD_AUDIO & MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS (Optional): Used only for real-time peer-to-peer audio transmission during live matches if voice chat is enabled. Audio is never recorded, stored, or processed on our servers.
  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES (Optional): Used solely for selecting a custom profile picture avatar.

2. How We Use Information

We use the data we collect solely for legitimate gaming operations:

  • Providing, updating, and maintaining multiplayer matchmaking and gameplay mechanics.
  • Syncing game progress, coins, and equipment securely across your devices.
  • Delivering advertisements through Google AdMob to keep the core game free-to-play.
  • Processing in-app purchases safely and verifying digital items.
  • Detecting, preventing, and addressing fraud, cheating, and security issues.
